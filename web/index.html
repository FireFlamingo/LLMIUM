Project & demonstration

From a parameter to measured behavior.

LLMIUM is a Rust workbench for inspecting open-weight checkpoints, making controlled edits, reproducing their effects and verifying restoration.

How to demonstrate it

  1. Open Weight explorer and inspect a registered checkpoint.
  2. Select a tensor and a bounded weight tile. Hover over a connection to identify its coordinate, then click to lock the selection.
  3. Enter a finite value in the selected-connection editor. Choose a quick probe or full-model generation and create an experiment.
  4. Follow progress, open the resulting evidence and compare reference, candidate and repaired results.
  5. Use Evidence & repair for the preserved real Qwen execution traces.

All edits create checkpoint copies in the local run directory. Full-model comparisons can take several minutes on CPU. Quick probes use synthetic inputs and measure only the selected vector or matrix.
